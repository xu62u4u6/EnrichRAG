## Design Context

### Users
EnrichRAG is used by bioinformatics engineers and wet-lab engineers who need to move quickly from a gene list and disease context into interpretable evidence. They are not casual dashboard users; they are technically fluent, evidence-sensitive users who need to explore known biology and use that foundation to surface unknown or less obvious insights.

### Brand Personality
The product should feel trustworthy, understatedly elegant, and mysterious. It should communicate scientific rigor without looking like a generic analytics dashboard or a hospital information system. The tone should be quiet, precise, research-native, and slightly exploratory.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light mode comes first. The palette should use low-to-moderate saturation with muted biological and archival tones rather than bright clinical colors or neon tech accents. The interface should avoid standard SaaS dashboard patterns, hospital portal visual language, and generic card-grid layouts. It should feel closer to an annotated research instrument, a refined lab notebook, or an evidence atlas than an admin console.

### Design Principles
1. Make evidence feel spatial and discoverable: show relations, citations, provenance, and uncertainty as explorable material rather than flat dashboard metrics.
2. Keep confidence quiet: use restraint, clear hierarchy, and precise typography instead of high-saturation status colors or loud effects.
3. Add mystery through structure, not decoration: use layered depth, reveal interactions, graph details, and archival/scientific textures sparingly.
4. Prioritize fast scientific work: the gene-to-insight path should stay direct, with advanced detail revealed progressively.
5. Avoid medical-admin familiarity: no hospital-blue palette, generic KPI cards, repeated icon-heading-text cards, or conventional enterprise dashboard chrome.
